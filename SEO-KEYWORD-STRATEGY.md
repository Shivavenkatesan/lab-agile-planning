## Technical SEO checklist
- Connect Google Search Console and analytics.
- Create XML sitemap and robots.txt.
- Use HTTPS, mobile-friendly layouts and readable URLs.
- Add unique page titles, meta descriptions and headings.
- Write descriptive image alt text.
- Link relevant category, product and enquiry pages.
- Add canonical tags and hreflang where applicable.
- Avoid duplicate, thin country pages.
- Add real contact details, privacy policy and terms.
- Connect and test the enquiry form before launch.
- Track organic sessions, target-country traffic, qualified enquiries and conversion rate.

## Six-month roadmap
1. Month 1: technical audit, keyword research, competitor review, analytics and indexing.
2. Month 2: optimize core and category pages.
3. Month 3: publish accurate product pages and buyer guides.
4. Month 4: improve internal linking and relevant industry partnerships.
5. Month 5: refine country pages and optimize based on Search Console.
6. Month 6 onward: report rankings, organic leads and conversions.
