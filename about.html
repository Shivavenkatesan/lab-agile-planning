About Aero Prime Med

A healthcare sourcing partner with a global outlook.

Aero Prime Med is a medical supplies brand focused on helping professional buyers explore healthcare products and communicate procurement requirements efficiently.

Our approach

Medical procurement involves more than finding a product. Buyers need clear specifications, accurate product information, appropriate documentation and responsive communication.

Our website brings relevant product categories together in one place for healthcare organizations, distributors, wholesalers and institutional buyers.

Our mission

To make medical product sourcing more accessible through clear information, customer-focused communication and professional procurement support.

Our vision

To build lasting business relationships in the medical supply sector and develop a trusted presence in markets we are equipped to serve.

Our values

  • Integrity and honest communication
  • Customer-focused sourcing support
  • Attention to product information and applicable documents
  • Professional and responsible business practices
  • Continuous improvement of catalogue and service
